DV Manipulation
- in the mod manager, you can select your
  DVs for optimal, hidden power types,
  and optimal shiny combination (Grass or
  Dragon). Apply DVs within the Party Menu
  submenu.
  
Decapitalization
- read the mod title

DexNav
- can be initiated from the Start Menu. Will
  populate with Grass, Rod, Dungeon encounters.
- register button doesn't currently work

Dynamic Scaling
- attempts to scale enemy trainer Pokemon to
  player's average party level with 3 difficulty
  settings, randomizer options, and as of 1.0.2
  now includes a toggle to keep the trainer's
  party roster size vanilla or full
- level caps can be toggled

Easy Mode
- applies buffs to the party Pokemon and debuffs
  to the opposing party Pokemon
- every attack from the player will be multi-hit
  and attacks like tomorrow Rollout or Triple
  Kick will gain power with each hit
- only real use case is testing mods from
  beginning to end

Evo Fix
- all different evolution method Pokemon will
  evolve after exhausting their learnsets
-after evolution, a prompt will appear going over
  moves the Pokemon would have learned at lower
  levels

Field Moves
- use TM/HM field moves if the trainer has the
  required badge and TM/HM
- Party Menu submenu will populate with all
  available field moves a Pokemon can learn
  as long as the trainer has the TM/HM

Limit Break
- breaks the level 100 cap for player and
  opposing trainers
- level caps will adjust based on badges and
  certain story events and even minor events
  like beating a trainer, getting a rematch call
  or getting a phone number
- works with Dynamic Scaling

Perfect Catch Rate
- 100% catch rate for any Pokeball type

TMs HMs
- reusable TMs and forgettable HMs
- will populate the TM pocket in numerical order

Team EXP Share
- modern EXP share mechanic
- fighters get 100% EXP, benched Pokemon receive
  50% exp

Trainer Rematch
- speak to any trainer, even gym leaders, and
  a prompt will pop-up asking if you accept a
  rematch
  * rematch pop-up is triggered by pressing A
    while standing next to the trainer
